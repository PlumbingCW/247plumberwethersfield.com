Commercial Plumbing on the Silas Deane Highway, Wethersfield, CT

Silas Deane Highway is Wethersfield's main commercial road. This page covers plumbing for the shops, offices and restaurants along it. It is a business district and not a residential neighborhood.

Silas Deane Highway

The corridor

Silas Deane Highway is Route 99. Public road references say it was built in 1930, that it enters Wethersfield from Rocky Hill as a four-lane road and that it continues into Hartford as Wethersfield Avenue. The town's 2013 plan describes the corridor as roughly three miles long and mostly commercial, with development essentially complete.

A 2006 market assessment of the corridor counted 166 parcels in the Wethersfield portion. Of those, 112 were commercial, 24 were single-family homes, 7 were multi-family and 3 were condominiums. That study is two decades old, so treat the numbers as a snapshot. The point stands: one street can mean a retail unit, an office suite, a restaurant or a house, and each calls for different questions.

Plumbing work in Connecticut follows the State Building Code, which adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC). Commercial fixtures, drains and water heaters are inspected against that published standard.

Water main work on the highway

MDC began replacing water mains, services and meter pits along Silas Deane Highway in April 2025. The work covers about 1.3 miles, from Parsonage Street in Rocky Hill to Mill Street in Wethersfield, and was projected to finish in August 2026. Check themdc.org for the current status.

Work on a nearby main can briefly change pressure or stir up sediment. After a service connection is changed, run the cold taps for a minute and clean the aerators if you see grit. If pressure stays low in a building, call and we will check whether the cause is inside or outside.

Permits and inspections

The Wethersfield Building Department is at 505 Silas Deane Highway and can be reached at 860-721-2840. It handles permits and inspects plumbing work. Commercial jobs often raise permit questions, and the rules vary with the work, so confirm your job with the department. We explain what a job involves so your question to the department is specific.
