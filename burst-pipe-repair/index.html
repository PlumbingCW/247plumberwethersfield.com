What to do when a pipe bursts

1

Stop the water

Close the main valve. If it will not turn, call us at once and keep everyone away from the flow.

2

Drain the line

Open the lowest faucet and flush the toilets once to empty the pipes.

3

Protect power

Keep out of rooms with water near outlets and cords. Shut that circuit off only if it is safe.

4

Call and document

Phone us and take photos. We will talk you through the rest.

Why pipes burst

Freezing

Water expands as it freezes and splits the pipe wall. The break often shows when the ice melts, not when it forms.

Corrosion and age

A pipe thinned by years of corrosion can fail without warning, especially at a joint or a bend.

Pressure spikes

A failed pressure reducing valve, a water hammer shock or a stuck valve can overstress weak pipes.

Damage

A nail, a drill bit or a renovation mishap can nick a line that fails days later.

How we repair a burst pipe

We locate the break, cut out the damaged section and replace it with sound pipe and proper fittings. Where several joints are corroded, we explain whether a longer run should be replaced. A burst that comes from a freeze needs a check of the nearby runs, because other sections of the same line often cracked too.

After the repair, we restore pressure slowly and check every joint for leaks. If water has reached insulation, drywall or flooring, we tell you plainly what should be dried, removed or left alone, and we recommend you call a water damage company for that work. We are plumbers, and we say so.

Frozen pipe, not yet burst

If a pipe is frozen but not broken, open the faucet, warm the pipe gently with a hair dryer starting near the faucet, and never use an open flame. If you see a crack, a bulge or water, shut the main valve and call us.

What winter means for Wethersfield pipes

About 27 percent of Wethersfield's housing was built before 1950, which means a good many homes have older supply lines that run through outside walls and unheated corners. Those are the runs most at risk in a hard freeze. Insulating them and keeping the heat on in unoccupied rooms costs little compared with a burst.

MDC advises customers to shut the main valve nearest the meter after a break and to call a plumber. It also notes that outdoor faucet shutoffs are usually within about 3 feet of the interior basement wall. Closing and draining that line in fall is one of the cheapest ways to prevent a spring burst. Do it before the first hard freeze.

When the burst is in the main line

A break between the street and the meter is MDC's responsibility, and MDC lists a Command Center line at (860) 278-7850, press 1, to report a main break or service leak. A break after the meter is generally the property owner's, and MDC can confirm the exact boundary for your address. Our water service line repair work covers the pipe that runs underground to the house. We help you tell which side of the meter the break is on, because that decides who fixes it.

Code and permit note

A burst pipe repair replaces damaged pipe with new material, and that work follows the plumbing code. The 2022 Connecticut State Building Code adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC), so ask the Wethersfield Building Department at 860-721-2840 whether the repair needs a permit.
