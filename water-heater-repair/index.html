When a water heater is an emergency

Safe first steps for a leaking or dead water heater

Hot water, gas and electricity are all present near a tank.

  • If the tank is leaking, close the cold water valve on the pipe above it and open a hot tap to relieve the pressure.
  • For an electric heater, switch off its breaker. For a gas unit, turn the control to pilot or off.
  • If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before you call us.
  • Do not remove the relief valve or its discharge pipe. Water that is hot enough to scald may come out.

Why water heaters stop working

No hot water at all

On an electric unit this is often a tripped breaker, a failed upper element or a bad thermostat. On gas it can be a pilot, a thermocouple or a gas control problem.

Hot water that runs out fast

A failed lower element or a heavy layer of sediment cuts the usable volume. The tank is still working, just poorly.

Popping or rumbling

Sediment at the bottom traps water and boils it. Flushing can help, and a tank that is badly scaled may be near the end.

Water around the base

Check the fittings, the relief valve and the drain valve first. If the tank shell itself is leaking, repair is not possible.

What we check before recommending anything

We confirm the fuel and the power first, because a surprising number of calls end at a breaker or a gas valve. Then we test the thermostat, the elements or the burner, the thermocouple or igniter, the relief valve and the venting. We also look at the age on the label, because a repair that costs more than half the price of a new tank is rarely a good bet.

If the repair is sensible, we do it and test the unit before we leave. If it is not, we explain why and quote a replacement so you can compare. You decide with both numbers on the table.

What is different about water heaters in Wethersfield

MDC states that its water has a hardness of about 1 grain per gallon, which is soft. Scale is a smaller part of the story here than in hard-water towns, so early failures tend to be corroded tanks, worn anodes or failed controls, not heating elements caked in minerals. That is good news, because it makes most repairs simple.

Many Wethersfield homes are single-family houses with a basement, and the water heater sits there near the main shutoff. That makes a leak easy to spot and easy to isolate, but a basement floor drain does not always handle a full tank. Know where your main valve is before you need it.

Heat pump and tankless units

Heat pump and tankless water heaters fail differently from a standard tank. Heat pump units can have fan, condensate and refrigerant-side faults. Tankless units usually need descaling or have a flow or ignition fault. Our tankless page explains those systems, and a tankless unit that is repeatedly shutting down is often a scale or venting issue worth a visit.

Code and permit note

Repairs that touch the relief valve, the discharge pipe or the venting are where code matters most. The state's 2022 building code adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC), which sets expectations for those parts. A like for like part swap is usually maintenance, and permit rules vary with the work.
