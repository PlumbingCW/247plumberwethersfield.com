This policy covers Wethersfield 24/7 Plumbing, located at 512 Silas Deane Highway, Wethersfield, CT 06109. Last updated November 14, 2026.

What we collect

When you call, we collect your phone number and the details you give us about the job, such as your address and the problem. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you email us, we keep the message and your reply address.

This website has no forms and no accounts. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and working.

How we use it

We use your information to schedule and complete plumbing work, to follow up on a job, and to improve how we answer calls. We do not sell your personal information.

Who may see it

Information goes to the plumbers and dispatchers working on your job and to service providers who help us run the phone line and website. We may share information when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us not to record a call by telling us at the start, and you can ask us to delete the details we hold about you, unless we must keep them for legal or accounting reasons. Write to contact@247plumberwethersfield.com to make a request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
