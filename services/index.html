Not sure where your problem fits?

Water in the wrong place usually belongs under burst pipe repair or leak detection. Slow or noisy drains belong under drain cleaning. Cold showers point to water heater repair. A wet basement after rain is a sump pump question first.

Still unsure? Call (860) 397-8103 and describe what you see. A few questions usually point to the right service, and the call itself is answered at any hour, so you never have to wait for the office to open.

For anything urgent, such as a pipe that is spraying or a sewer that is backing up, do not wait to read further. Shut off the water at the valve nearest the meter if you can, then call the emergency line and we will talk you through the next step while a plumber is sent.

For planned work, such as a new water heater or a repipe, call us in the daytime and we will schedule a visit and explain the price before anything starts. Work that changes pipes or fixtures follows Connecticut's State Building Code, which adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC), and permit rules vary with the work.
