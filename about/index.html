Wethersfield 24/7 Plumbing exists for the moment something goes wrong at home and you need a real person to answer.

We work from 512 Silas Deane Highway in Wethersfield, Connecticut. That address matters because the highway is the town's main commercial road, and from it we can reach Old Wethersfield, the Berlin Turnpike side and the neighbors in Rocky Hill, Newington, Hartford and Glastonbury without long drives.

How we work

You call, and the line is answered whatever the hour. We ask a few questions, help you stop the damage, and send a plumber who looks at the problem before anything is priced. You approve a clear price before work starts. If the job grows once a wall or floor is open, you hear about it first.

We do not pretend every call is an emergency. A dripping faucet can wait for morning, and we will say so. A burst pipe or a sewage backup cannot, and those calls move to the front of the line.

What we will and will not say

We are licensed and insured, and we encourage you to verify any plumbing contractor yourself. Connecticut's Department of Consumer Protection runs a license lookup at elicense.ct.gov, and you can see whether a license shows as active or approved. We do not publish star ratings, review counts or invented testimonials on this site, because we would rather you judge us on the first phone call.

We also avoid promising an exact arrival time. Weather, traffic and active emergencies change the answer from hour to hour. What we promise is an honest window for your street when you call.

What a first call sounds like

You will not be handed a script. The person who answers asks where you are, what you can see and whether water is still moving. If it is, the first thing we do is help you find the shutoff. That single step prevents more damage than anything a plumber can do an hour later, so we start there every time.

From there we describe what is likely going on, what the visit will involve and how pricing works for that kind of job. We explain which parts of the price depend on what the plumber finds, so a number does not change on you without a reason.

What we handle

Our work falls into a few groups. Water problems include leak detection, burst pipe repair and water service line repair. Drain and sewer work includes drain cleaning, hydro jetting, sewer inspection and sewer line repair. Hot water work covers repair, replacement and tankless units. We also handle sump pumps, basement waterproofing, boilers and commercial plumbing.

How we price

We do not print dollar figures on this site, because the right number depends on your house, your pipes and the hour of the call. We explain the factors that move the price and give you a number once a plumber has seen the problem. If you want a second opinion, you are free to get one.

Local information we rely on

Wethersfield gets its water and sewer service from the Metropolitan District Commission, and the town Building Department at 505 Silas Deane Highway handles permits. We link to those public sources on our guides and area pages, and we say plainly when a rule varies, so you can confirm it with the people who set it. The state's current building code adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC), which is the reference a licensed plumber works from.

What to expect when the plumber arrives

The plumber starts by looking, not by quoting. They check the fixture, the shutoff and the area around it, and they tell you what they see in plain words. Then the price is explained, including what could change it, and you decide whether to go ahead. Nothing starts until you agree.

When the work is done, the plumber shows you what was changed, tells you what to watch for over the next few days and cleans up the work area. If a part needs ordering or a second visit is needed, you hear that before the plumber leaves.

Questions about our company

We keep this page short on slogans for a reason. A plumbing company earns trust by answering the phone, explaining the work and standing behind it, not by listing adjectives. If anything on this site is unclear, ask when you call, and we will answer plainly.
